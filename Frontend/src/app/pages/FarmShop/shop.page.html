Collection & delivery

Collect from the barn, or we’ll bring it round

Our van does two rounds a week across the vale. Everything else waits for you in the shop chiller with your name on it.

Collect for free

  • Any time the farm shop is open, from 10am
  • Order by 8pm for collection the next day
  • Park in the yard; your order is in the chiller by the till

Local delivery

  • Tuesday and Friday rounds, 9am to 5pm
  • £3.50, or free on orders of £45 and over
  • Minimum order £20; meat arrives frozen in wool-lined boxes
