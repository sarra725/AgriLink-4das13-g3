Our story

Three generations on one muddy lane

Brookmoor was 60 acres of dairy grazing when Arthur and June Pennock bought it in 1962. Their son Tom dug the first market garden in the eighties and sold what he grew from a trestle table at the gate.

Today Tom’s daughter Hannah runs the farm with her partner Sam and a crew of nine. We grow more than sixty crops a year in no-dig beds, keep Red Ruby Devon cattle and Poll Dorset sheep on the old pasture, and have planted four miles of new hedgerow for the birds and the bees.

  1. 1962Arthur and June buy a 60-acre dairy holding
  2. 1987First veg stall on a trestle table at the gate
  3. 2009First 40 veg boxes go out on a Thursday
  4. 2017The whole market garden moves to no-dig beds
  5. Now140 acres, 380 box households, a farm shop in the barn
