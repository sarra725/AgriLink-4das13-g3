Furrow — Free Bootstrap 5 Farm Website Template by uiCookies
https://uicookies.com/

Furrow is a free website template for a family-run mixed farm with a farm shop,
veg box subscriptions and pick-your-own fields, shown as the fictional Brookmoor
Farm. It ships three pages in deep green, straw yellow and soil brown on cream,
with Zilla Slab headings, Figtree body text and a few hand-drawn touches. The
home page has a seasonal hero, an in-season calendar with a month picker, a veg
box builder with a live price, farm shop hours with an open-now status, a
pick-your-own board, the farm story, events with a booking modal and a
newsletter. The shop page has category filters and a basket drawer saved in
localStorage, with collection or delivery and a postcode check. The visit page
covers picking, prices per kg, an illustrated farm map, opening times,
directions, accessibility, dogs and an FAQ. Everything runs on vanilla
JavaScript with no jQuery, all content is visible without JavaScript, and
animation respects prefers-reduced-motion.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html            Home page: seasonal hero, in season now, veg boxes,
                        opening hours, pick your own, story, events, newsletter
  shop.html             Farm shop: filters, sorting, basket drawer, delivery check
  visit.html            Picking guide and prices, farm map, opening times,
                        directions, accessibility, dogs policy, FAQ
  css/vendor/           Bootstrap 5.3.8 (minified, self-hosted)
  css/style.css         Template design layer (palette, type, components)
  js/vendor/            Bootstrap 5.3.8 bundle (includes Popper)
  js/main.js            Template behaviour — vanilla JavaScript, no jQuery
  img/                  Demo photos (public domain, see CREDITS.txt)
  CREDITS.txt           Source and licence for every demo photo

NOTES
  - Built on Bootstrap 5.3.8. No jQuery, no plugin dependencies.
  - Colours and fonts are CSS custom properties at the top of css/style.css.
  - The seasonal produce calendar, delivery postcodes, delivery fee and
    minimum order are small settings at the top of js/main.js.
  - Farm shop hours are read from the list in the footer (data-open and
    data-close on each day), so the open/closed status, today's row and the
    collection days all follow when you edit it.
  - Forms, bookings and the basket are front-end only; point them at your
    form service or shop platform to make them live.
  - Replace the demo images in img/ with your own optimised photos before launch.
